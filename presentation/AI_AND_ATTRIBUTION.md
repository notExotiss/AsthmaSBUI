# AI, data, and attribution notes

Review this document with the student team before submitting. The Congressional App Challenge rules require disclosure of AI use and expect students to demonstrate substantial personal effort and understanding of the app's technical work.

## AI used in the running app

- The chat assistant calls Gemini through the authenticated Firebase callable function `askAboutRecords` in `functions/index.js`.
- The function reads records for the signed-in user's UID, keeps the Gemini key server-side in Secret Manager, and asks the model not to diagnose or recommend medication changes.
- The risk chart is generated by `buildRiskTrend` in `environment.ts`; it is a local, recency-weighted model, not a Gemini prediction.

## AI used during development

Codex was used as a coding assistant during this project, including to edit and refine application code, user-interface behavior, measurement/relay integration, charts, and these presentation materials. The team should review the commit history, add any other AI tools used, describe the assistance accurately in the submission, and be ready to explain and demonstrate the code. Do not describe AI use as limited to wording or brainstorming if it also contributed code.

## Originality and prior code

The repository's `README.md` says the app layout starts from the MayoCodes GitHub version. The challenge rules say the submission must be original and solely created by the contestant, and require new coding to be highlighted when submitting a 2.0 version. Before entering, the team should document which code and assets came from the earlier repository, verify its license and the challenge's eligibility requirements, and identify the team's own work. Do not claim the entire codebase was written from scratch by the team.

## Demo-data provenance

`firebase-data.ts` contains helpers named `seedSampleMeasurements`, `seedHistoricalRiskReadings`, and `seedRiskForecastCheckins`, and the app invokes these for a designated demo account. If any displayed history was created by those helpers, do not describe it as captured by the physical sensor. Use newly captured readings for the live measurement demonstration and identify generated history as example data if it appears on screen.

The Community map also uses configured example pins, zones, and report counts over real map geography. They are not actual community submissions.

## Claims to avoid

- Do not call the airflow result validated spirometry or a clinical peak-flow reading.
- Do not call the risk output a medically validated forecast or diagnostic result.
- Do not imply that illustrative map reports were submitted by real residents.
- Do not expose account credentials, API keys, or private health information in the video or repository.
